# Privacy Policy — The Tower (PHS Tower app)

**Version 1.0**\
**Effective date:** July 10, 2026\
**Last updated:** July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how *The Tower*, the student newspaper of Princeton
High School ("The Tower," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information in the
**PHS Tower** mobile app for iOS and Android (the "app").

The app is published under the bundle/application ID `org.princetonk12.phstower`.

If you have any questions about this policy, email
**phstowersenioreditors@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. The short version

- The app is free, contains **no advertising**, and contains **no third-party
  analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs**.
- You can **read the entire newspaper without an account**. Signing in is
  optional and is only required for the Outreach and Schedule sections.
- Sign-in is limited to **@princetonk12.org school accounts**.
- We **do not sell or rent your personal information**, and we do not use it for
  advertising or share it with data brokers.
- We do not request access to your **location, camera, microphone, photo
  library, contacts, calendar, health data, or files**.
- The only personal information we deliberately collect is (a) your Google
  account name, email address, and profile picture when you choose to sign in,
  (b) the class schedule information you choose to enter in the Schedule
  section, and (c) the content of anything you choose to submit to us, such as
  a Letter to the Editor.
- If you use the Schedule section, your schedule is **private by default**.
  Your name is shown to classmates **only if you turn on sharing**, and only to
  classmates who have also turned on sharing.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional. If you tap "Sign in with Google," Google sends us:

- your name (display name),
- your email address,
- your Google profile picture, and
- a Google account identifier and sign-in token.

We use this to create and maintain your account with our backend provider,
Supabase, so the app can recognize you across sessions and so submissions you
make can be attributed to you. We do **not** receive your Google password, and
we do not gain access to your Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google service.

Sign-in is restricted to **@princetonk12.org** Google accounts issued by
Princeton Public Schools (plus designated Tower staff accounts). Other Google
accounts are refused and no account record is kept for them.

You may sign out at any time from the account menu in the app's masthead.

### 2.2 Content you submit to us

If you use **Letter to the Editor**, we store the subject line and body of your
letter, together with the name and email address on your signed-in account, so
that our editorial board can read, verify, and consider your letter for
publication. The app tells you on that screen that your name and email are
attached automatically.

Please do not include information in a letter that you would not want our
editors to read, and be aware that letters selected for publication may appear
in *The Tower* in print or online, normally with your name.

Other forms in the app (for example the "Join the Tower" interest form and any
surveys) are **Google Forms** displayed inside the app. Responses to those forms
go to Google and to the Tower staff account that owns the form; they are covered
by Google's Privacy Policy in addition to this one.

### 2.3 Class schedule information (only if you use the Schedule section)

The Schedule section is optional and requires sign-in. If you use it, we store
the schedule entries you type in yourself: for each class period, the teacher
you select, which semester(s) the class runs, and an optional course name. This
information is **self-reported by you** — the app has no access to Princeton
Public Schools' student records, registration systems, or official rosters.

Your schedule is **private by default**. Nobody else can see it until you turn
on the "Share my schedule" switch, and:

- sharing only ever reveals your **name and profile picture**, and only to
  signed-in PHS students who have entered **the same class section** (same
  period and teacher) *and* have turned on sharing themselves — visibility is
  mutual, so nobody can see classmates without being visible too;
- your email address, your other classes, and your schedule as a whole are
  **never** shown to other students, whether or not you share;
- you can turn sharing off at any time in "My Classes," which immediately
  removes you from classmates' lists.

Schedule entries are kept for the school year they belong to and are deleted
when the new school year's calendar is loaded (normally each August). You can
also edit or remove individual entries at any time in the app.

The bell schedule and school calendar shown in this section are school-wide
reference data maintained by Tower staff; they are the same for everyone and
contain no personal information.

### 2.4 Push notification information

The app uses **OneSignal** to deliver push notifications about new issues and
stories. When the app starts, OneSignal registers your device and processes:

- a push token and a OneSignal device identifier,
- basic device information such as device model, operating system version,
  language, and time zone,
- your IP address, from which an approximate (typically country- or
  region-level) location may be derived, and
- a subscription tag we set (`audience = all`) so editors can send general
  announcements.

We use this only to send and route notifications, and to know how many devices
are subscribed. We do not use it to build advertising profiles. You can decline
the notification permission when prompted, or turn notifications off at any time
in your device's system settings for the PHS Tower app.

### 2.5 Information stored only on your device

The following stay on your device and are never transmitted to us:

- **Saved articles / bookmarks** — stored locally so your reading list works
  offline.
- **Game state**, such as crossword and Minesweeper progress.
- **Cached images and PDF pages**, so the app loads faster and uses less data.
- **Your sign-in session token**, if you are signed in.

Deleting the app removes all of this local data from your device.

### 2.6 Information collected automatically by our providers

When the app fetches articles, images, issues, or fonts, our service providers
receive ordinary technical information required to serve a network request,
including your **IP address**, device type, and the time of the request. This
happens with:

- **Supabase**, which hosts our article database and article images/PDFs,
- **Google Fonts**, from which the app loads certain typefaces at runtime,
- **Google**, for sign-in and for any Google Form shown in the app, and
- **OneSignal**, as described above.

We do not use this technical information to identify individual readers.

### 2.7 Information we do not collect

The app never asks for a location permission and contains no location-tracking
code. It does not collect or request: GPS or device location, camera or
microphone access, your photos, your contacts, your calendar, health or fitness
data, financial or payment information, browsing history outside the app, or
device identifiers used for advertising (IDFA / Android Advertising ID). The app
displays no ads and contains no ad SDK.

The one exception to "no location" is the approximate, country- or
region-level location that OneSignal may infer from your IP address, described
in section 2.4. That is a by-product of your device connecting to the internet
rather than something the app measures or asks permission for, we do not use it
to target or identify you, and we do not receive any more precise location than
that.

> Note: the "camera" in the app's Vanguard reader is a purely software view
> control that pans and zooms the page image. It does not use your device's
> physical camera, and the app never requests camera permission.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

- show you the newspaper and let you save, search, and share articles;
- authenticate you and keep you signed in when you choose to sign in;
- show you your own schedule and, **only if you opt in**, show your name and
  profile picture to classmates in the same class section who have also opted
  in;
- receive, review, and respond to letters and form submissions you send us;
- send notifications you have opted into;
- keep the app secure, diagnose crashes and bugs, and prevent abuse; and
- comply with the law and with school district policy.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or
trade it.

## 4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that make the app work, and
only to the extent needed:

| Provider | What it handles | Its privacy policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase, Inc.** | Article database, images/PDFs, accounts, letters | https://supabase.com/privacy |
| **Google LLC** (Sign-In, Forms, Fonts) | Sign-in, embedded forms, fonts | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| **OneSignal, Inc.** | Push notification delivery | https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy |
| **Apple Inc. / Google LLC** (app stores) | App distribution and push transport | Apple / Google policies |

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where
Princeton Public Schools policy or law requires disclosure — for example, in
response to a lawful request, or to address a credible threat to someone's
safety.

**Other students:** if — and only if — you turn on "Share my schedule," other
signed-in PHS students who are in the same class section as you *and* have
also turned on sharing can see your name and profile picture in that class's
classmates list. This is the only information the app ever shows other
students about you, and turning sharing off removes it.

Members of *The Tower*'s editorial board and its faculty advisers can read
letters and form responses submitted through the app, as part of normal
newsroom operations. A small number of Tower technical staff administer the
app's database and, like any service administrator, are able to access stored
account and schedule records when maintaining the system; they are not
permitted to use that access for anything other than operating the app.

## 5. Data retention

- **Account records** are kept while your account exists, and are deleted on
  request.
- **Schedule entries** are kept for the current school year and deleted at the
  yearly rollover (normally each August). You can edit or delete them yourself
  at any time in the app.
- **Letters and form submissions** are kept as part of the newspaper's
  editorial record. Published material remains published; unpublished
  submissions are deleted on request where we are able to do so.
- **Push notification records** are kept while your device stays subscribed.
  Uninstalling the app or disabling notifications ends the subscription.
- **On-device data** stays until you clear it or uninstall the app.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can, at any time:

- **Read anonymously** — do not sign in; the news, games, and Vanguard sections
  do not require an account.
- **Keep your schedule private** — the Schedule section works fully without
  sharing; the "Share my schedule" switch in "My Classes" is off by default
  and can be turned off again at any time.
- **Turn off notifications** — decline the prompt, or disable notifications for
  PHS Tower in your device settings.
- **Sign out** — tap your avatar in the masthead and choose "Sign out."
- **Delete on-device data** — uninstall the app.
- **Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and any
  submissions**, by emailing **phstowersenioreditors@gmail.com**. We will
  respond within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days. Deleting your
  account removes your profile record and all of your schedule entries and
  unsubscribes you from Tower services; it does not retract material already
  published in the newspaper.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as
the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, including rights of
access, correction, deletion, portability, and the right to object to certain
processing. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context
behavioral advertising as those terms are used in California law. To exercise
any right, use the contact address above; we will not discriminate against you
for doing so.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: **consent** (notifications,
optional sign-in), **legitimate interests** (running and securing the app,
publishing the newspaper), and **legal obligation** where applicable.

## 7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for Princeton High School students, their families, and the
surrounding community, and is rated for users **13 and older**. It is not
directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided
us personal information, email **phstowersenioreditors@gmail.com** and we will
delete it promptly.

Because many of our readers are minors, we deliberately keep collection to a
minimum: no ads, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no location data.

## 8. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers uses standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.
Accounts are authenticated through Google and Supabase rather than through
passwords we store ourselves — we never see or hold your Google password.
Access to submitted letters is limited to Tower editors and advisers.

Schedule data is protected by database-level access rules: your schedule
entries are readable only by your own signed-in account, and classmates lists
are computed on the server so that the only information ever released to
another student is the name and profile picture of someone who has opted in.
No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

---

## 9. Where your information is processed

Our service providers (Supabase, Google, OneSignal) operate servers in the
United States and may process and store information there and in other
countries. If you use the app from outside the United States, you understand
that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.
